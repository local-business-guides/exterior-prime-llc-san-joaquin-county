Questions worth asking before you choose

A brief conversation can help you understand whether the service is a good match. Ask questions such as:

  • What cleaning method do you use on roofs?
  • How do you keep the approach suitable for the roof rather than treating it like a driveway?
  • Which parts of the roof are included in the requested work?
  • Is gutter cleaning a separate service I should request?
  • What should I move or keep clear before the visit?

Be ready to describe the property

You don’t need a technical roof report to request information. Explain what you can observe from the ground, such as where the discoloration appears or whether you’re also interested in cleaning gutters. If you know of a recent repair, delicate area, or access limitation, bring it up early. A company can then consider that information when discussing the work.

Keep the conversation focused on the service you need. If your main concern is roof appearance, say so. If you’re also planning driveway or solar panel cleaning, mention those as separate requests and ask which services the company provides. Confirm the scope for your particular request rather than assuming everything is included automatically.

Watch for shortcuts in the hiring process

Avoid picking a company solely because it appears first in a search or is closest on a map. Also be cautious about agreeing to roof work when you haven’t been told what method will be used.
