Share useful details before the visit

A few clear details can make scheduling and planning easier. List the surfaces you’re concerned about, point out visible stains or buildup, and mention whether the work area is behind a gate or near delicate landscaping. If you want several areas cleaned, name each one instead of assuming that a general house-washing request covers the driveway, roof, panels, and gutters too.

Move personal belongings and vehicles away from the work area when possible. Let the provider know about pets, access restrictions, or anything fragile nearby. You can also ask whether you need to be home during the work and what the crew needs available. Getting those answers beforehand helps prevent confusion on the day of service.

Avoid assumptions about the scope

A common mistake is expecting one service name to cover every task around the home. Exterior cleaning methods differ by material, and some work may need a separate conversation. Don’t assume that a roof, solar array, gutters, and siding will all be treated the same way. Ask what’s included, what method is planned, and whether any areas need special access.
